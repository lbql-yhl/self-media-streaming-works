Privacy approach for your Velon tasting notebook

This Policy explains how Velon handles information when you plan food-first trips: choosing tastes, browsing curated cities and stops, drafting routes, saving wishlist places, writing visit notes, and building a digest album on your device.

Product: Velon
Scope: Mobile App features that run primarily on your device
Account model: No registration or sign-in is required
Privacy desk: ember.lane@velonpalate.co

Why this Policy exists

Velon is built as an on-device food planning notebook. We describe the categories of data the App may process, the reasons for that processing, the permissions that may appear, how local storage works, and the choices available to you. We avoid absolute promises that cannot be verified, and we do not claim that Velon never processes any data at all.

Data that may appear inside your planning workspace

Depending on how you use Velon, the App may process:

  • Taste preference tags you select to rank cities and tasting stops.
  • Route drafts, stop order, day assignments, wishlist entries, and custom city or custom place details you enter.
  • Visit notes and optional photos you attach to notes or upload as custom city covers.
  • Local status values needed for ordinary App function, such as whether a place is wishlisted or when a draft was last updated.
  • Technical signals the operating system provides for standard App operation on your device.

Velon does not require a name, email, or password to use the notebook. If you email us, we will receive the information you include in that message so we can respond.

Purposes tied to food-route planning

We process the information above to:

  • Match curated cities and stops to your taste tags and show why a stop may fit.
  • Persist route drafts, wishlist items, custom places, and visit notes so you can continue planning across sessions on the same device.
  • Render digest previews and allow you to share album cards when you initiate sharing.
  • Maintain App stability, fix defects, and respond to privacy questions you send to our desk.
Effective date notice: this Privacy Policy takes effect on September 20, 2026. If you use Velon after that date, this Policy applies to that use.

Permissions the App may request

When you choose to upload a custom city cover or attach a photo to a visit note, Velon may request access to your photo library. Granting that permission is optional. If you decline, you can still use preference selection, discovery, routing, wishlist, and text notes that do not need images. Velon does not need continuous background location tracking or a camera permission to perform its core planning flows.

Where planning materials live

Preferences, drafts, wishlist data, visit notes, and related files are stored locally on your device using the App's storage areas, including documents storage for optional images. Keeping materials on-device helps the notebook work without requiring you to create a cloud profile. Device backups you configure yourself (for example through your operating system) may include App data according to your device settings.

Network activity and leaving the device

Core city and spot packets are packaged with the App for local use. If you use the system share sheet to send a digest card or image, content you select may leave your device and be handled by the destination you choose. Those destinations follow their own privacy practices. Aside from communications you initiate (such as emailing our privacy desk), Velon is not designed around continuous uploading of your tasting notes to our servers.

Third parties that may appear at the edges

Operating system services (such as the photo picker or share sheet) may process information according to platform rules when you invoke them. We do not sell your planning notebook contents. If a future update introduces additional third-party components that process personal data, we will update this Policy so the description remains accurate.

Practical safeguards

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to an on-device planning App, including limiting processing to what the features need and relying on platform storage protections. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. You can reduce risk by keeping your device updated, using device passcodes, and avoiding sharing digest cards that contain photos you prefer to keep private.

How long materials remain

Local planning data generally remains until you delete a note or place, clear App data, or uninstall Velon. Photos you attached remain in the App documents area while associated records exist. Correspondence you send to our privacy desk is retained only as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable laws and relevant regulatory requirements.

Choices available to you

You may change taste preferences at any time, remove wishlist items, delete visit notes, skip photo attachments, or revoke photo library access in system settings. Because Velon does not operate a mandatory cloud account, the primary way to remove on-device notebook data is through in-App deletion actions or by uninstalling the App. For questions about these choices, contact ember.lane@velonpalate.co.

Younger travelers

Velon is not directed at children who are unable to consent to digital services under applicable laws. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children for marketing profiles. If you believe a child has provided information to us through email in a way that should be removed, please write to us and we will take appropriate steps.

Policy updates

We may revise this Policy when features, permissions, or legal expectations change. Updated text will be made available in a clearly identifiable way. Continued use of Velon after an update means the revised Policy applies to subsequent use, except where applicable laws require a different approach.

Governing standards for privacy questions

Privacy-related questions and complaints will be addressed under applicable laws and relevant regulatory requirements. This Policy does not name a specific country, region, court, or arbitration body. Where mandatory local rules give you additional rights, those rights remain available.

Write to the tasting privacy desk

If you have a privacy request, clarification need, or concern about how Velon handles notebook data, email ember.lane@velonpalate.co. Please include enough detail for us to understand the issue. We will respond within a reasonable period.
